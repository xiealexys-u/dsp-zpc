Modelling choices

  • Two levels. The overview is the general process to discuss with Cyberdune and use for context in Deliverable 1. The detailed model is for analysing the slice you choose. Each coloured overview step bundles the decision points shown in its label.
  • Reference model, not Cyberdune's current process. Check in the client interview which of these tasks their agents already do and where their sign-off sits.
  • One candidate from "Application received" onwards. The vacancy-level part runs once, and the flow after it shows the path of one applicant. A fuller model would use a multi-instance subprocess.
  • Every knock-out and score passes D7 in this map. Whether an agent may skip that step is the delegation question itself, so the map keeps it open.
  • Decision log. Every D-point is assumed to write the rule applied, the evidence used, the actor, a timestamp and the model or rule version. This record is what your 2-minute reconstruction criterion tests.
  • Legal hooks are pointers, not legal advice. Under the Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) the AI Act's Annex III duties apply from 2 December 2027. Full sources are in the References below.
